# ACC Cleaner Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 September 2026

This policy describes the privacy behavior of ACC Cleaner (`com.acc.cleaner`) for the v1.1.0 Play-release baseline.

## Local processing

ACC Cleaner examines files and folders on the user's Android device to provide file-maintenance and cleanup features. File names, paths, metadata and file contents used for preview remain on the device.

The v1.1.0 baseline does not request Android's INTERNET permission and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. The app therefore does not transmit personal data, file contents, file lists, device identifiers, analytics events or advertising identifiers to the developer or to third parties.

## File and folder access

On Android 11 and later, the optional device-wide Scan Perangkat feature and the WhatsApp Business Cleaner may use Android's `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` special access. This permission is used only for the core purpose of locating, reviewing and managing files/folders in shared storage.

The general File Cleaner also provides Folder Scan through Android's Storage Access Framework. With Folder Scan, the user selects a folder through the Android system picker and the app receives access only to the selected scope.

## Deletion behavior

ACC Cleaner does not automatically select or delete files. Scan results are shown for review. Permanent deletion occurs only after the user selects files and confirms deletion through the app's confirmation flow.

## WhatsApp Business Cleaner

The WhatsApp Business Cleaner searches supported WhatsApp Business storage locations for temporary/log files and large files that may require review. Bulk selection only marks matching files; it does not delete them automatically.

ACC Cleaner is not affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp or Meta.

## Advertising and analytics

The v1.1.0 baseline contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. If a future release adds advertising, analytics, subscriptions or other network services, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration must be updated before that release is published.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when application behavior changes. The public privacy-policy URL used in Google Play must always match the behavior of the version distributed to users.
